  1. Generate an Ed25519 key pair. Commands for Linux, macOS and Windows at /en/join.
  2. Connect to ssh entre@runv.club and submit your public key through the terminal form (English available).
  3. The queue is reviewed manually. Once approved, the account is created and confirmation is sent by email.
