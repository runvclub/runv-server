
  runv.club — join request
  ─────────────────────────

  This is a Brazilian pubnix: a shared Unix shell, a page at ~/public_html,
  curious people, and a calmer internet.

  You'll be asked a few **straightforward questions** next — on each screen,
  read the title and type your answer **on the indicated line** (Enter to
  confirm; one step lets you use several lines and finish with a line that's
  just a single dot: .)

  We'll ask for: your desired username, email, sites or profiles where you
  show up online (blog, code, networks you use), and your **public** SSH key.

  The request goes into a queue; the team handles each case manually — **there
  is no instant account**.

  Ready? Press Enter on the next screen and we'll get started.
