
  Before the SSH key
  ───────────────────

  In the last step you'll paste **only the public key** — a single line,
  starting with something like ssh-ed25519 or ssh-rsa.

  **Never** the private key (files without .pub, lines starting with
  -----BEGIN … PRIVATE KEY-----, or a PuTTY "Private key" export).

  If in doubt, generate a fresh pair on your computer before pasting.
